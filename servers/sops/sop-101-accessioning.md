# SOP-101 Specimen receipt and accessioning
Version 4 | Effective 2026-02-01 | Owner: Histology supervisor

## 1. Purpose and scope
Covers receiving surgical and biopsy specimens into histology, matching them to their requisitions, and creating the accession in the LIMS. Applies to every specimen container and requisition that arrives at the histology receiving bench, including STAT and after-hours deliveries.

## 2. Identifier check at receipt
Every container must carry two patient identifiers that match the requisition exactly, plus the specimen site and the collection time. Check the container against the requisition before scanning anything. The accession is created only after both identifiers, the site and the procedure match. Unlabeled containers are never accepted.

## 3. Labeling discrepancies
If the container and requisition disagree on any identifier or on the specimen site (for example left versus right), do not gross or process the specimen. Flag the specimen in the LIMS with the reason "labeling discrepancy" and a description of what differs, place it in the discrepancy rack, and notify the submitting clinic by phone. Histology staff never correct or relabel a container themselves; only the submitter can resolve a discrepancy, and the resolution is documented in the LIMS before work resumes.

## 4. Recording fixation and ischemia times
Record the time the specimen was placed in fixative and, for breast specimens, the cold ischemia time (removal from the patient to placement in fixative) as written on the requisition. If either time is missing for a breast specimen, request it from the submitter before the specimen is processed; these times decide whether ER, PR and HER2 results are reportable (see SOP-120).

## 5. STAT specimens
STAT specimens are accessioned within 15 minutes of arrival, ahead of routine work, and placed in the red STAT rack. The accessioning tech sets priority to STAT in the LIMS so the specimen appears on every downstream worklist as STAT.
