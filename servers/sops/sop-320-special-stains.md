# SOP-320 Special stains
Version 3 | Effective 2026-02-12 | Owner: Lead histotech

## 1. Purpose and scope
Covers the special stains GMS, AFB, PAS and Trichrome on the special stains stainer SS-01.

## 2. Control tissue for each special stain
Every special stain run must include a positive control slide stained alongside the patient slides: GMS uses fungal control tissue, AFB uses tissue containing acid-fast mycobacteria, PAS uses glycogen-rich tissue such as liver or kidney, and Trichrome uses tissue with collagen such as liver or uterus. Patient slides are released only if the control in the same run shows the expected staining.

## 3. GMS
GMS is a silver stain and over-staining is the common failure: watch the control under the microscope during silver impregnation and stop when fungal walls are black and the background is pale. Use acid-cleaned glassware only.

## 4. AFB
AFB slides are read under oil immersion by the pathologist, so cut sections for AFB at 4 micrometers on clean slides with no water bath contamination; tap-water organisms can cause false positives. Use filtered water for the floating bath on AFB days.

## 5. Turnaround
Special stains ordered before noon are stained the same day; later orders are stained the next morning. STAT special stain orders are run as soon as SS-01 is free.
