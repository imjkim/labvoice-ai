# SOP-610 Instrument downtime and status changes
Version 2 | Effective 2026-03-15 | Owner: Histology supervisor

## 1. Instrument statuses
Online: available for patient work. Maintenance: planned or vendor service. QC hold: failed QC, see SOP-420. Offline: broken or unplugged. Anything other than online means the instrument must not be used for patient slides.

## 2. Who may change a status
Any tech may request a status change through LabVoice or the LIMS, but only the supervisor approves putting an instrument into maintenance, QC hold or offline, or bringing it back online. The request must include a note saying why.

## 3. Logging downtime
For maintenance, record the vendor, the work being done and the expected return time in the instrument note. Update the note if the estimate changes. Every status change is kept in the instrument log with who approved it.

## 4. Coverslipper jams
If the coverslipper CS-01 jams, stop it, remove the slide rack, and check every slide in the rack for wet mountant or misaligned coverslips. Recoverslip affected slides by hand. A coverslipper that jams twice in one shift is put into maintenance.
