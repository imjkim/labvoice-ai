# SOP-710 Slide QC, labeling and release
Version 3 | Effective 2026-01-20 | Owner: Lead histotech

## 1. Slide quality check
Before release, check each slide for complete tissue, no folds or tears through the area of interest, adequate staining and a clean coverslip without bubbles over tissue. Slides that fail are recut or restained before release.

## 2. Slide labels
Each slide label must show the accession, block and stain, and its barcode must scan to the same case in the LIMS. Scan the slide and its block together at the release station; a mismatch stops the release until resolved by the lead tech.

## 3. Releasing slides to the pathologist
When every slide for the case passes QC, set the case to slides ready in the LIMS and deliver the slides in the assigned pathologist's tray, STAT cases on top. Partial releases are allowed only for STAT cases, with a note listing what is still pending.
