# SOP-420 IHC instrument QC failure and QC hold
Version 2 | Effective 2026-04-01 | Owner: Histology supervisor

## 1. Purpose and scope
What to do when an IHC stainer (IHC-01 or IHC-02) fails its daily QC or a run's controls point to an instrument problem.

## 2. What QC hold means
An instrument on QC hold must not stain patient slides. Slides already stained in the failed run are quarantined in the QC hold tray and are not released to pathologists. Orders assigned to the instrument stay open in the LIMS, so the affected cases show as blocked.

## 3. Steps after a failed QC
1) The tech who ran QC records the failure in the LIMS with what was wrong, for example a weak HER2 positive control. 2) The supervisor puts the instrument on QC hold in the LIMS. 3) The tech replaces reagents and repeats QC with fresh controls. 4) If the repeat QC passes, the supervisor returns the instrument to online. 5) If it fails again, the supervisor calls vendor service and the instrument stays on hold.

## 4. Rerouting STAT work
While one IHC stainer is on hold, STAT IHC orders move to the other IHC stainer if it is online. The supervisor reassigns them in the LIMS; techs do not move orders themselves. If no IHC stainer is available, the supervisor notifies the assigned pathologist of the delay for every STAT case.

## 5. Returning to service
An instrument returns to online only after a passing QC run is recorded. The LIMS refuses to bring an instrument online while its most recent QC is a failure.
