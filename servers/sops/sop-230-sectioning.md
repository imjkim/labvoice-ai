# SOP-230 Embedding and sectioning
Version 4 | Effective 2026-02-20 | Owner: Lead histotech

## 1. Purpose and scope
Covers embedding processed tissue in paraffin blocks and cutting sections on the microtomes MT-01 and MT-02.

## 2. Section thickness and water bath
Cut routine H&E and IHC sections at 4 micrometers; kidney biopsies at 3 micrometers. Float ribbons on the water bath at 40 to 45 degrees Celsius and pick them up on charged slides for IHC. Label every slide with the accession and block before it leaves the microtome station.

## 3. Blade safety and injuries
Lock the handwheel and engage the blade guard whenever your hands are near the blade, including when changing blocks. Remove blades with the magnetic tool, never with fingers, and discard them in the sharps container. If you cut yourself, stop work, apply first aid, and report the injury to the supervisor before the end of the shift; contaminated blocks and slides from that moment are recut.

## 4. Sectioning problems
For chatter or thick-and-thin sections, cool the block face on ice, check the clearance angle and change the blade. For holes or crumbling tissue, the specimen may be under-processed: tell the lead tech before cutting further, because the block may need reprocessing.

## 5. Recuts and levels
Recuts, levels and unstained slides are cut only from a pathologist's order in the LIMS. Cut levels at the spacing the order specifies, and number the slides in cutting order.
