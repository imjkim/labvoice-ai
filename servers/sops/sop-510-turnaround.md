# SOP-510 Turnaround time and escalation
Version 3 | Effective 2026-02-01 | Owner: Histology supervisor

## 1. Turnaround targets
Routine biopsies: slides to the pathologist within 2 working days of receipt. Resections: within 4 working days. STAT specimens: within 1 working day. Add 1 working day when IHC or special stains are ordered. Due times in the LIMS are calculated from these targets.

## 2. Overdue cases
The lead tech reviews the LIMS overdue list every morning at 9:00. For each overdue case, record the reason in the LIMS and the step it is waiting on. Overdue cases sitting in slides ready are delivered to the pathologist first.

## 3. Escalating blocked STAT cases
If a STAT case is blocked for more than 2 hours by an instrument problem, a failed QC or a missing reagent, the tech tells the supervisor, and the supervisor notifies the assigned pathologist. Record the notification in the LIMS.

## 4. Communicating delays outside the lab
Only the supervisor or the pathologist contacts clinicians about delays. Techs do not call clinicians about results or turnaround, and never give results over the phone.
