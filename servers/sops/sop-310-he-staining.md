# SOP-310 H&E staining
Version 6 | Effective 2026-01-05 | Owner: Lead histotech

## 1. Purpose and scope
Covers routine hematoxylin and eosin staining on the automated H&E stainer HE-01 and the manual backup method.

## 2. Daily H&E control slide
At the start of each day, stain a control slide before any patient slides. Check that nuclei are crisp and blue, cytoplasm shows shades of pink, and there is no residual water or bubbling. Record the result in the LIMS. If the control slide is unacceptable, do not stain patient slides until the problem is corrected and a new control passes.

## 3. When HE-01 is down
If HE-01 is in maintenance or offline, stain on the manual H&E line at the backup station. Stain STAT slides first. Manual timings: hematoxylin 5 minutes, rinse, differentiate 10 seconds, bluing 1 minute, eosin 1 minute, then dehydrate, clear and coverslip. Mark manual-run slides "manual H&E" in the LIMS so the pathologist knows the method.

## 4. Reagent changes
Change hematoxylin every 1,500 slides or 7 days and eosin every 1,000 slides or 7 days, whichever comes first. The stainer's reagent counter is checked at the start of each shift.
